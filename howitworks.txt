The Goods Availability Checker and Inventory Management System is a web-based application designed to provide real-time information on the availability of goods in nearby shops. It also allows shopkeepers to upload and update their inventory, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information for customers.
In the current market scenario, customers often face difficulties in finding the availability of specific goods in nearby shops, leading to frustration and wasted time. Shopkeepers also struggle to keep their inventory information updated and accessible to potential customers.
              To create a user-friendly web application that provides real-time availability of goods in nearby shops and to enable shopkeepers to easily upload and update their inventory and to improve the shopping experience by reducing the time and effort required to find desired goods.
       The system is developed using modern web technologies, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and a backend framework such as Node.js or Django. A relational database like MySQL is used to store inventory data and user information. The application features a responsive design to ensure compatibility across various devices.
         The system consists of two main components:
User Interface: Allows customers to search for goods based on their location and view availability in nearby shops.
Shopkeeper Interface: Enables shopkeepers to manage their inventory by adding, updating, and deleting goods from an easy-to-use platform for managing their inventory, leading to increased customer satisfaction and sales.
		        Conclusion :The project aims to bridge the gap between customers and shopkeepers by offering a reliable and efficient solution for finding and managing goods. Future enhancements may include additional features such as real-time notifications, advanced search filters, and integration with payment gateways.
